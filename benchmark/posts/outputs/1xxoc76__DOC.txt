When people start a business they usually plan for the obvious stuff: inventory, rent, marketing. Those get a line in the budget. It's the smaller, less visible costs that seem to catch people off guard later.

A few I keep thinking about:  
- Payment processing fees (they add up fast)  
- Packaging  
- Returns  
- Maintenance  
- Taxes

For those of you who've been running a business for a while: which single expense surprised you the most? And what would you have budgeted for from day one if you could go back?
