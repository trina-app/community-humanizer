I run a small creative studio on my own, and lately admin is eating about half my week. Sorting client files, tracking which revision is the current one, chasing quotes that never got a reply. By the end of a busy week I've done a lot of work and very little of it was billable.

The context-switching is the part that hurts most. I'll be deep in a project, an email comes in, and twenty minutes later I've answered it, filed an attachment, and lost my train of thought. Getting back into the work takes longer than the email did.

I've tried a couple of things. Morning time-blocking helped a bit, and so did a stricter folder structure. But in a busy week the admin still piles up faster than I can clear it, and both fixes seem to fall apart.

For other solo owners or tiny teams: how do you structure your week to protect billable hours? Specifically, do you batch admin into one dedicated day, or handle it as it arrives? If you batch it, how do you keep things from going stale (especially quotes) while they wait? And if you handle it as it comes in, how do you keep it from wrecking your focus?

I'd love to hear what actually works in practice, including things that didn't work.
