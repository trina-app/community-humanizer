Went to an AI keynote today and kept hearing the same thing in the slides. "X does Y. Z doesn't." Like "checking every line of code vs. every decision" or "blaming the team vs. taking ownership." Once I noticed it I couldn't stop. Then I started seeing it on ads, street banners, websites, decks, everywhere.

My guess is it's Claude-style phrasing leaking into how people talk and write. Some of it probably isn't AI at all and just happens to fit the pattern, but I still get this gut feeling of "Claude wrote that" even when I can't point to a specific pattern.

I like the tool, I use it. But it worries me a bit that AI is shaping how people communicate and even think. When I write something that actually matters I want it to sound like me. I kinda think the people who keep writing on their own are going to stand out and come across as more authentic.
