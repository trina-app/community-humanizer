Been a CFO at a few startups and I've watched strong and weak ones raise, and honestly it comes down to something pretty simple. VCs fund proven demand or a proven team. Mostly that.

What they want is evidence you can return big money. Uber in 2011 had a San Francisco pilot, real revenue, repeat riders, and you could copy it city by city. Airbnb in 2009 had people already looking on Craigslist and Couchsurfing, and demand spiked when big events sold out hotels.

Team version: Beehiiv founders built Morning Brew's growth engine, sold to Business Insider for $75M. Affirm was Max Levchin, PayPal and Slide.
