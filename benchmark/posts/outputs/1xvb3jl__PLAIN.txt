I hired an SEO agency in June and I think I got burned. I'd like to hear from people who've been through this, and what I should have asked before signing.

The agency is HigherVisibility. I signed up for their "SEO + AI Search Visibility" plan at $2,550/month plus a setup fee. All in, I've paid roughly $16k so far.

Here's what I have to show for four months:

- No changes to my website
- No content and no community posts
- 8 backlinks total, all low quality (Before It's News, APSense)
- Domain rating is still 2
- AI visibility is flat

Meanwhile the reports and the dashboard kept saying we were making progress. The numbers I could verify didn't move.

I tried to cancel on Oct 9. The contract requires 60 days' notice with no early buyout option, so I'm locked in and paying through Dec 8. I should have read that clause harder before signing, and that one's on me.

What I'm hoping to get from this thread:
