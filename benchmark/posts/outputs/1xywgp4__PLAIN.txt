I run a retro gaming site off an M4 Mac Mini at home. It has an 8TB SSD and a 10Gbps line, and it serves about 140 TB a month. The only way in is a Cloudflare tunnel, with Cloudflare's caching and bot protection in front of it, and so far that has worked great.

What worries me is DMCA. Cloudflare forwards the notices they receive about the site, and I answer every one of them quickly. Even so, I can't shake the feeling that at some point they'll decide the account isn't worth the hassle and drop it. I'd rather have a fallback ready than scramble after it happens.

My constraints:

- At home I only have a shared IPv4, so I can't port-forward directly.
- I don't want my home IP exposed to the public.
- Going IPv6-only is technically possible, but I'd rather not. It cuts off too many visitors.

So I'm looking for a cheap or free alternative to the tunnel. One idea is a VPS as a front end, with a tunnel or WireGuard back to the Mac Mini, but I'd need one with at least 2Gbps of unmetered bandwidth, and I don't know which providers offer that at a sane price (or whether "unmetered" holds up in practice at this volume).

Has anyone here set up something like this? Do you have recommendations for providers, or for a different approach altogether? Anything that has worked for you, or bitten you, with this much traffic would be really helpful.
