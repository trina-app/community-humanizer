I went to an AI keynote today and kept hearing the same pattern on the slides: "X does Y. Z doesn't." Things like "Checks every line of code. Not every decision." or "Blames the team. Doesn't take ownership." Once I noticed it I couldn't stop. It's on ads, street banners, websites, pitch decks, everywhere.

I'm pretty sure it's Claude-style phrasing leaking into how people talk and write. Some of it probably isn't AI at all and just happens to fit the pattern, but I get this gut feeling of "Claude wrote that" even when I can't point to a specific tell.

What worries me is that AI is shaping how we communicate and even how we think. I like the tool, I use it every day. But when I write something that actually matters, I want it to sound like me. I think the people who keep writing on their own are going to stand out, and feel more authentic.

Anyone else noticing this? Or am I just ranting like the guy in They Live with the sunglasses on?
