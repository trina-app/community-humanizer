We sold our business a while back after years of building it from nothing. The buyers paid the initial down payment, and we agreed to monthly installments for the rest. They haven't made a single one since. Not one.

The business is still running and still taking in customer revenue, so it's not like there's no money coming in. They have cash and just aren't paying us.

We've already spent thousands on legal attempts to collect, and so far it's gotten us nowhere. At this point I'm worn out. Every dollar going to legal fees is a dollar that was supposed to come from the sale, and I'm starting to wonder if chasing it is costing more than it's worth.

If you've been in this spot, I'd really like to hear from you:

1. How did you actually get paid without legal costs eating up whatever you recovered? Is there a point where it makes sense to settle for less, or a cheaper route than a drawn-out lawsuit?
2. For anyone who has done a seller-financed sale, what would you put in place up front to protect yourself? Security interests, personal guarantees, a down payment large enough to cover the risk, anything else?

I wish I'd known to ask these questions before signing, so I'm hoping this helps the next person too. Thanks for reading.
