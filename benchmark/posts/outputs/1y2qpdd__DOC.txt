Long Claude answers got hard for me to navigate. By the time I scrolled to the end I'd forgotten what I was looking for. So I added a standing instruction: end every reply with a short "## 80/20" block, always placed last, under a divider line.

It has fixed lines with emoji labels:

* 🎯 **Result**: always there
* ➡️ **Next**: only when relevant
* ⚖️ **Decide**: only when relevant
* ⚠️ **Watch out**: only when relevant
* 🌿 **Git**: only when relevant

The key actions are in bold, so if you read only the bold text you already have the answer. Here's an example from my hours-tracker app:

\---

## 80/20

🎯 **Result:** Timer bug fixed, entries now save in the right timezone.

➡️ **Next:** **Run the migration** on the dev DB.

⚖️ **Decide:** **Round to 15 min or keep exact minutes?**

⚠️ **Watch out:** Old entries keep the wrong timezone until migrated.

🌿 **Git:** **Commit** on the `fix/timezone` branch.

I saved it in two places: claude.ai user preferences, and CLAUDE.md for Claude Code. That way it applies everywhere, including the really long chats about the hours-tracker app I'm building, where I used to get lost the most.
