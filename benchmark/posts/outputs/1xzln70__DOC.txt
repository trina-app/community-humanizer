What I've tried so far:

* Posts on Reddit and X
* A launch thread or two
* Directory listings

Each one gets some views and a few signups, then it fades out. Nothing I've done is something I could repeat on the next project.

I keep reading generic playbooks ("talk to your users", "build in public", "do things that don't scale"), and I'm not after more of those. I'd like real, specific accounts from people who've been through this.

**What I'd like to know:**

* Where did your first 10 users actually come from? Not the advice, the real answer.
* Did unscalable outreach (DMs, cold email) pay off for you, or was it a time sink?
* Were early paid ads worth it, or did you burn money for nothing?
* What did you stop doing because it was a waste of time?

I'm especially interested in B2B, since that's where I struggle most. With consumer stuff I at least get a trickle of signups. With B2B I get close to nothing, and I don't know whether it's the product, the channel, or me.

If you've got a story, even a messy one, I'd love to hear it.
