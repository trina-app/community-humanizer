Long Claude answers got hard for me to navigate. I'd scroll through six paragraphs just to find out what I actually needed to do next.

So I added a standing instruction: end every reply with a short **## 80/20** block, always last, under a divider line. It looks like this:

```
---
## 80/20
🎯 Result: Fixed the timezone bug; entries now save in UTC.
👉 Next: **Run the migration** on the staging DB.
⚖️ Decide: **Keep per-day rounding or switch to per-entry?**
⚠️ Watch out: Old entries won't be converted automatically.
🌿 Git: Commit on a new branch, **don't push to main yet**.
```

A few details that make it work for me:

- **Result** is always there. **Next**, **Decide**, **Watch out** and **Git** only show up when they're relevant, so the block stays short.
- The key actions are in bold, so if you read only the bold text you still get the answer.
- It's always at the bottom, so I know where to look, and I can skip the rest unless I want the details.

I saved it in two places: my claude.ai user preferences, and CLAUDE.md for Claude Code. It now applies everywhere, including the long chats about the hours-tracker app I'm building, where it helps most. After a long back-and-forth I can jump to the block and pick up where I left off.

It's a small change, but it's the one that made long sessions much less tiring.
