Been a CFO at a few startups and watched strong and weak ones raise. Honestly it boils down to something pretty simple: VCs fund either proven demand or a proven team. That's mostly it.

What they want is evidence there's a high chance of a big return. Demand you can generate and repeat at scale, a market whose appetite is growing, or founders who've already won before.

Uber 2011: SF pilot, real revenue, repeat riders, and you could copy it city by city. Airbnb 2009 had demand already sitting on Craigslist and Couchsurfing, plus spikes when big events sold out hotels. Beehiiv 2021, the founders built Morning Brew's growth engine, sold to Business Insider for $75M. Affirm 2014 was Max Levchin, PayPal and Slide.

Not promoting anything btw
