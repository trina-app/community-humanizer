I'm totally blind and based in the UK. With the new age-verification laws coming in, I've been thinking a lot about how much of what I use depends on third-party services, and how little say I have in the terms they decide to impose. It's made me want to cut back on that dependence where I can.

Self-hosting seems like the obvious route. I like the idea of not being tied to anyone's platform, and of running things on my own terms instead of waiting for the next policy change to land on me.

What I don't know is how realistic that is with a screen reader. So I'd love to hear from anyone who's done it, especially other blind or visually impaired self-hosters:

- **Setup:** How did you get things installed and configured? Was it mostly command line, and did you run into anything that was a pain to do without sight?
- **Administration:** How accessible are the web UIs and dashboards for the stuff you run? Are there projects that are particularly good or particularly bad for screen reader users?
- **Day-to-day use:** Once everything is up and running, how does it hold up for regular use and maintenance?

Any advice on where to start, what to avoid, or which tools play nicely with a screen reader would be really appreciated. And if you're a blind self-hoster, I'd be glad to hear how you got into it and what your setup looks like.

Thanks in advance.
