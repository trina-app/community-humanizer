I'm an early-stage founder building a platform that collects real-world audio and video data from wearables like smart glasses. The idea is that robotics, embodied AI, and multimodal AI teams need first-person, real-world data, and that it's hard to get at scale. We have 150+ registered contributors and a basic working product.

What we don't have is confirmation that any researcher actually wants this data, let alone would pay for it. I haven't had a single conversation with someone who would be a buyer, and that's the part that worries me.

**What I've tried so far**

- Cold emails to professors and authors of relevant papers. Zero replies.
- Looking into conferences. Most cost $1,000+ once you add the ticket, travel, and lodging, which feels like too much to spend before I've validated anything.
- Trying to find where the people who actually use these datasets hang out. I haven't found a good answer.

**What I'm trying to figure out**

1. If you've sold to hard-to-reach technical buyers (researchers, ML engineers, academics), how did you get your first few customer conversations once cold email failed?
2. Are expensive conferences worth it this early, or is that something to save for after you've validated?
3. What are cheaper ways to get in front of these people? I've wondered about writing content (technical posts, a small dataset release, something useful to the community) or joining or building a community where they already gather. Has anything like that worked for you, or is it a time sink at this stage?

I'm not looking to promote anything. I just don't want to keep building before I know whether anyone wants this. Any advice, especially from people who've been in a similar spot, would be appreciated.
