Went to an AI keynote today and the slides kept using the same rhetorical pattern. "X does Y. Z doesn't." Over and over. Checking every line of code vs. checking every decision. Blaming the team vs. taking ownership. After that I started seeing it everywhere: ads, street banners, websites, decks.

Some of it probably isn't AI at all, it just fits the pattern. But I got that gut feeling of "Claude wrote that" even when I couldn't point to anything specific.

I like the tool. But I'm worried it's shaping how people talk and write, and maybe how they think. When I write something that actually matters, I want it to sound like me. My guess is people who write in their own voice will stand out more and come across as more authentic.

Does anyone else notice this? It feels like They Live. Once you see it you can't unsee it, and now I'm seeing it everywhere.
