We're a bootstrapped team and we launched Stitch.ai last week, after years of building it. It's an AI-native all-in-one workspace for solo entrepreneurs and small business owners: email, calendar, tasks, notes, contacts, files, sheets, slides, boards and forms, all in one place.

**What we think makes it different**

* **AI that reads everything.** It auto-extracts entities, tags and connections from every item, so you don't have to organize things by hand.
* **Manual linking across apps.** You can link any item to any other, like backlinks, so an email, a task and a note can all point at each other.
* **Knowledge Hub.** It gathers everything around a contact in one place: emails, notes, tasks, files.
* **Stitchy, the assistant.** It drafts replies, creates tasks, writes briefings and follow-ups. You control what gets imported, so it only sees what you let it see.
