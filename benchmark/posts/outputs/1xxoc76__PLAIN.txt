I've been thinking about how most of us plan for the obvious stuff when we start out: inventory, rent, marketing. Those are the line items everyone budgets for because they're the first things you think of.

What seems to catch people off guard is the stuff that doesn't show up until you're a few months in. Payment processing fees that quietly eat a few percent of every sale. Packaging costs that add up faster than you'd expect. Returns, which cost you the product, the shipping, and your time. Maintenance and repairs on equipment. Taxes, especially if you didn't set money aside as you went.

For those of you who've been operating a while: which single expense surprised you the most? And knowing what you know now, what would you have budgeted for from day one?

I'm curious whether the answers cluster around a few common culprits or whether it varies a lot by type of business.
