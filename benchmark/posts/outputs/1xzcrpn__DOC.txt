I run a one-person creative studio, and I'm at the point where roughly half my week goes to admin instead of actual project work. Sorting client files, keeping track of which revision is the current one, chasing quotes that are still pending. None of it is hard, but it eats the hours I'm supposed to be billing.

The constant switching is what really kills my focus. I'll be in the middle of something and an email comes in about a file, and then I've lost the thread.

I've tried a couple of things. Time-blocking the mornings for project work helped a little, and so did setting up a stricter folder structure. But in a busy week the admin still piles up anyway.

For other one-person or tiny-team owners: how do you structure your week so billable hours stay protected? Do you batch all the admin into one day, or deal with it as it comes in? Curious what's actually worked for you.
