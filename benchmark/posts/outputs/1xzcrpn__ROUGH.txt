I run a one-person creative studio and about half my week now goes to admin instead of actual project work. Sorting client files, keeping track of which revision is which, chasing quotes that are still pending. The billable stuff gets whatever is left.

The context-switching is what kills me. I'll be in the middle of something and an email comes in that needs a file found or a quote followed up, and it takes a while to get back into it. I've tried time-blocking mornings and a stricter folder structure. Both helped a little, but in a busy week the admin still piles up anyway.

For other solo owners or tiny teams: how do you structure your week to protect billable hours? Do you batch admin into one day, or deal with it as it comes in?
