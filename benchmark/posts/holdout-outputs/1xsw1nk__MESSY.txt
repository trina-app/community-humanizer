Everyone says talk to customers early and I did, but I'm not sure I like what I heard lol. 5 weeks in, MVP is built, and I've talked to about 20 potential clients. Almost all of them were positive on the call. Then they get cagey and say they'd want to see a more mature product first, come back when it does more, that kind of thing.

Only 2 of the 20 actually agreed to a pilot. And both of them want just one small feature. Not the thing I've been building toward, a side piece I honestly never thought was worth much. I added it almost as an afterthought.

So now I don't know what to do. Part of me wants to go all in on that feature since it's the only thing anyone's actually said yes to. But that's not why I started this, and it feels like I'd be building a completely different company. The other option is to keep building the full product and just lead with the feature when I talk to people, but is that just me being stubborn?

The pilots also mess with the business model. I had one in mind for the whole product, but if nobody wants to buy the whole thing, what am I even charging for? Do I price the one feature? Do I treat the pilots as a way in and hope they grow into the rest? No idea.

Two data points out of 20 isn't much to bet a company on, but it's also 2 more than I had a month ago. Has anyone been through this, where what people want is a tiny slice of what you set out to build? How did you cope with it, and did you pivot or hold on to the vision?
