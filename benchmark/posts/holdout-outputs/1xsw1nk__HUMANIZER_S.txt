I'm five weeks in and have an MVP built. I've talked to about 20 potential clients so far, and the feedback has been mostly positive. Nobody says it's a bad idea. But almost everyone holds back and says they'd want to see a more mature product before committing to anything.

Only 2 of the 20 agreed to a pilot. Both of them want just one small feature, something I built almost as an afterthought and never thought was the valuable part. The rest of the product, which is the whole reason I started this, doesn't seem to interest them.

So now I'm stuck between two options. One is to pivot fully and build the company around that one feature, which feels like giving up on the original vision. The other is to keep building the whole product and just lead with that feature when I talk to people. The second feels safer, but I'm not sure it's honest with myself or with them.

The pilots also make me rethink the business model. If nobody wants to buy the full thing, I'm not sure what I'd be charging for, or how much.

Has anyone been through this? How did you cope with realizing the market wants 10% of what you built? And how did you decide whether to follow the small thing or stick with the big thing? (I will not promote)
