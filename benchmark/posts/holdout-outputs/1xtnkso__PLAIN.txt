What I didn't do in that whole year was talk to a single customer.

A couple of months ago ChatGPT started referring random visitors to it. I can watch what they do on the site, which has been the closest thing to user research I've had. So far that's about 130 signups, running 2-3 a day. I turned on payments last week and have had zero purchases.
