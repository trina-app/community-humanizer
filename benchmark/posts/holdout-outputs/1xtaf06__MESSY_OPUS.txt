so the thing that surprised me most is how readable it is from across the room. 31.5 inches of colour e-ink is just big, it's a Fraimic Large Canvas and I finally got Tesserae running on it this week

Tesserae is the AGPL self-hosted e-ink dashboard server I maintain, runs in Docker or as a Home Assistant add-on, no account needed

two dashboards rotate on it right now

one is project stats for the last 30 days. 606 active installs (up from 342), 1,020 stars, 92 merged PRs, 68 of those from 8 outside contributors which honestly is the number I like looking at most

before anyone asks, the installs number is from opt-in telemetry. it's off by default and it's an anonymous count only, nothing else. if you don't turn it on nothing phones home, at all. I know people here care about that and so do I

other one is my github profile, contribution graph, streak, top repos, recent activity. bit vain maybe but it looks really nice in colour

I built both by just describing what I wanted to an AI agent through Tesserae's MCP server. but they're not some black box, they're plain HTML/CSS/JS with data sources so if you'd rather hand edit them you totally can

also you don't need a giant panel for this, it runs on cheaper stuff too, TRMNL, reTerminal, Inky Frame, old Kindles
